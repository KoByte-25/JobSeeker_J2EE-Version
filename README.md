Project Description
JobSeeker Workforce Management System is a J2EE web application designed to connect employers with job seekers across Myanmar. The platform serves two primary user groups: employers who post job opportunities and job seekers who search for employment.

For Employers:
Employers can create and manage job listings on the platform. However, all job posts must pass a security review by the JobSeeker Admin Team before appearing on the website. The admin team notifies employers via email regarding the approval or rejection of their job listings, whether they are creating new posts or editing existing ones. If a job posting is rejected, employers can revise and resubmit it with corrected information.

For Job Seekers:
Job seekers can browse available positions, submit applications, and track their application status. When a job seeker applies for a position, the employer who posted that job receives an email notification. Employers can review applications, select the most qualified candidates from the applicant pool, and arrange interviews. Additionally, employers can report applicants whose information appears to be inaccurate or fraudulent.

Communication and Notifications:
Job seekers receive email notifications when employers schedule interviews with them. This ensures clear communication throughout the hiring process.

Admin Oversight and Security:
The JobSeeker Admin Team continuously monitors both job postings and user accounts. When fraudulent job information is detected, the admin team rejects those listings immediately. Similarly, if job seekers are reported for providing incorrect personal information, the admin team sends warnings requiring them to update their profiles. If job seekers fail to correct their information after a very long time or after multiple warnings or receive numerous reports, their accounts may be permanently deleted from the platform.
